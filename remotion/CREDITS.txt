SCREENSHOTS
VistaVR: project screenshot already supplied in Wence Tongol’s portfolio
repository, github.com/codew3y/wencebenieremtongol.
TechSelect and BarberHouse: React-drawn, theme-aware in-film UI studies based
on the public product interfaces. BarberHouse’s light treatment is a film
adaptation, not a change to its deployed site.

GRAPHICS
Wire cube, orbital geometry, product frames, workflow diagram and motion
are drawn in React/SVG/CSS specifically for this film. No stock footage, music,
or generated imagery is used. The integration diagram is based on the public
portfolio project description, with no client data or invented client UI.

SOFTWARE
Remotion 4.0.535: https://remotion.dev
FFmpeg, libx264 and libvpx-vp9: provided by Remotion for browser video encoding.
